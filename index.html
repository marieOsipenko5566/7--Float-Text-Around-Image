There’s a surprising amount of variety when it comes to cat breeds. Some have short hair while others have long tresses that require regular hands-on grooming. Some cats weigh about four pounds; others can be up to 30 pounds. And of course, each cat has its own personality. Some breeds are known to be more affectionate while others are fiercely independent. No matter what your life looks like, there’s sure to be a cat here that would make a sweet addition to your household.
